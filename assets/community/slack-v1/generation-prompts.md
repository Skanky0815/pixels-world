# Pixel-Slack-Emojis: Bildquellen und Prompts

Mit dem integrierten Bildwerkzeug anhand des freigegebenen Pixel-Porträts und Idle-Masters erstellt. Die Originale in `sources/` bleiben unverändert; der Export reduziert auf ein klares 64×64-Raster und skaliert anschließend ohne Glättung auf 128×128.

Die drei ergänzten Motive für Fortschritt, Fragen und Ideen verwenden `pixel_love.png` als Identitäts- und Stilreferenz.

## pixel_approved

Quelle: `sources/pixel_approved.png`

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: ONE standalone custom Slack emoji, square, true transparent background. Generate Pixel, the exact grey-brown tabby cat from the two references (Image1 approved head portrait, Image2 game idle), as a compact head-and-one-paw approval reaction. Preserve white muzzle, white cheek/chest patch, pink inner ears/nose, dark tabby forehead M and stripes, green eyes, face shape, cute chunky pixel-art identity. Same three-quarter face toward the RIGHT as the portrait, slight confident smile, ONE large fluffy front paw raised beside the lower-left cheek, paw pads visible like an approval wave (real cat paw, NO human thumb/hand). Add one bold simple green CHECK MARK beside the raised paw, small enough that the cat remains dominant but clear at22px. The head and paw should fill about90% of the square with a small safe transparent margin. Crisp coarse pixel clusters on a logical64x64 grid, hard pixel outlines, restrained game palette, no soft shading, no antialias blur, no glow, no vignette, no floor, no drop shadow, NO BACKGROUND PIXELS, NO text, no letters, no borders, no logos. A single emoji, not a sheet or mockup. Designed to remain readable as a tiny22px Slack reaction. Output square PNG with alpha.
```
