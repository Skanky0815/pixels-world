## Animationsblatt

Input image 1 is the approved Sara character source and identity authority; image 2 is her approved 64x96 game raster and size/silhouette authority. Create ONE transparent PNG animation sheet, exactly 12 separate full-body poses in a clean SIX COLUMNS by TWO ROWS grid. Suggested output 1536x768, equal cells 256x384. Every cell is the SAME adult chibi woman in strict LEFT-FACING SIDE VIEW, same large head, short compact body, blonde relaxed ponytail with dark roots, two playful flyaway strands, green eye, dark framed glasses IN EVERY FRAME, subtly defined chin, charcoal hoodie, black ripped jeans and practical dark shoes. Preserve the approved identity and compact proportions. No tall or realistic adult proportions. Restrained chunky pixel art, crisp square clusters, 1 logical pixel outline, flat shared limited palette of about 24 colors, no painterly shading, no gradients, no glow, no shadows. Each figure has about 60 logical pixels of visible height, drawn at 4x enlargement; all figures exactly the same body/head scale. Leave generous transparent padding above. All shoe soles on a common baseline within their own cell (about y=360 in a 384px cell). The character is centered at x=128 in each cell. NO drawn grid, cell borders, words, numbers, captions, background, floor or props. Genuine transparency between figures and around every silhouette; fully opaque interior colors, hard alpha edges. Do not connect neighboring figures. Do not crop hair, glasses, arms or shoes. Frame order, left-to-right then next row: TOP ROW cells 0 neutral approved idle hands in hoodie pocket, 1 subtle idle exhale plus blink, 2 walking left first contact left leg forward, 3 walking left passing pose, 4 walking left opposite contact right leg forward, 5 walking left opposite passing pose. Reactions must be visibly distinct from one another by arm silhouette and posture, restrained dry character, never screaming or aggressive. Exactly twelve full bodies, no extra views, no cats. This is an animation sheet, not a scene or character concept board.
