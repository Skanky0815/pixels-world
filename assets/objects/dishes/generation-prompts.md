# Geschirr: Erzeugungsprompts

Modus: integriertes Bildwerkzeug (`image_gen`), je eine neue Grafik mit transparentem Hintergrund. Die unveränderten Quellen liegen in `sources/`.

## cup

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: ONE transparent low-resolution pixel-art game sprite for Pixel’s World, a warm cozy 2D side-view cat game.
Primary request: a small plain ceramic mug, warm ivory cream body with one simple muted bordeaux-red band just below the rim, a dark warm-brown outline, and one small open loop handle on the right. A shallow dark opening visible at the top, no contents or steam. Everyday slightly rounded sturdy cup for an old-fashioned kitchen.
Style: crisp square pixel clusters, restrained cream/bordeaux/walnut palette, authentic tiny hand-placed pixel art, no antialiasing or smooth gradients. Designed to read at 24 pixels wide by 26 pixels tall, including handle.
Composition: exactly one complete isolated mug centered, front/side view, flat horizontal base, entire object visible with ample true transparent alpha margin, handle hole genuinely transparent. No floor, no external cast shadow, no table, no cat, no lettering, no logo, no decoration other than the single colored rim band, no watermark, no other objects, no sprite sheet.
```

## plate

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: ONE transparent pixel-art game sprite for Pixel’s World, a cozy warm side-view cat game.
Primary request: one empty low ceramic dinner plate seen from the side, resting flat on its base. Warm ivory cream glaze, a single muted bordeaux-red narrow rim stripe, a dark warm-brown outline. A shallow elliptical top surface is visible, enough to read as a plate rather than a bowl. Matches a cream mug with a bordeaux band in an old-fashioned walnut kitchen.
Style: very crisp low-resolution pixel art with square pixel clusters and a small warm cream/bordeaux/brown palette; no gradients, no antialiasing, no realistic gloss. Designed for 32 pixels wide by 10 pixels high. It must be notably flatter than a bowl.
Composition: exactly ONE complete standalone plate centered, low horizontal silhouette width about three times height, flat horizontal base and very little depth. Genuinely transparent alpha background and generous clear margin around the plate. No table, no floor, no cast shadow outside the object, no food, no utensils, no cup, no second plate, no lettering, no logos, no watermark, no sprite sheet.
```

## shard

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: ONE tiny transparent pixel-art ceramic shard sprite for a 2D side-view cozy cat game.
Primary request: a single small irregular triangular broken piece of warm ivory cream ceramic, with a fragment of muted bordeaux-red rim stripe and a dark warm-brown edge. It belongs to matching cream crockery with a single burgundy rim band. A simple believable chipped piece, no whole cup or plate.
Style: crisp square pixel art clusters, very restrained cream/bordeaux/walnut palette, no smooth gradients, no antialiasing. Intended final size 10 by 8 pixels, chunky and readable.
Composition: exactly one isolated shard centered with transparent margin, roughly triangular, side-view decoration that can be rotated in code. Genuine transparent alpha background, no floor, no cast shadow, no multiple shards, no text, no logo, no watermark, no scene, no sprite sheet.
```
