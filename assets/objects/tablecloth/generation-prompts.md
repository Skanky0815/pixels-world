# Tischdecke: Erzeugungsprompts

Modus: integriertes Bildwerkzeug (`image_gen`). Das Stoffmuster ist deckend, das abgelegte Stoffknäuel besitzt echte Transparenz. Die zweite Erzeugung verwendet das Stoffmuster als Stilreferenz. Unveränderte Quellen unter `sources/`.

## Stoffmuster

Create one seamless flat repeating gingham cloth texture for a warm cozy 2D pixel-art kitchen game. Top-down flat textile pattern ONLY, fills entire square image edge to edge, opaque. Small regular checks in warm cream, muted bordeaux red and soft faded rose, dark red intersections. Very simple restrained 6-color palette, large crisp square pixel blocks, no gradients, no antialiasing, no realistic woven microtexture, no folds, no border, no fringe, no table, no room, no props, no text. The pattern will be sampled down to 32x32 game pixels with 2x2 pixel clusters, then tiled across a tablecloth's narrow edge and hanging end. Keep the checks perfectly axis-aligned and repeatable, warm hand-painted-game mood but strict pixel geometry.

## Abgelegter Stoff

Create ONE small production sprite for a cozy 2D pixel-art platform game, genuinely transparent background. A warm cream and muted bordeaux-red gingham TABLECLOTH crumpled in a low pile on the floor, no other objects. Match the gingham colors of the previous texture reference. Wide low irregular fabric heap, side view orthographic, long curled corner trailing toward the left, a few large readable folds and cream hem, dark warm red/brown pixel outlines. No floor, no ground shadow, no table, no cat, no dishes, no text, no backdrop. Compact whole cloth heap centered with padding. Intended final sprite112x36 game pixels with chunky 2x2 pixel clusters, simple readable pixel shapes, no antialiasing, no blur, no realistic woven microtexture. The cloth is a continuous thin fabric, not a cushion or pillow.
